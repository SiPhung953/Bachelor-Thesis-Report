Slide 3: Introduction - Why recruitment?

Let's look at the recruitment statistic in Vietnam.
- In 2024, our labor force is at approximately 52.9 million people.
- Nationwide survey shows that 35% of Vietnamese professionals are job hunting. Yet the hiring process remain slow: Another survey shows that the time between the application being sent and the job offer was accepted is a staggering 44 days. [This statistic, feels like conveying the idea that "I am addressing this problem with my product", but in reality the product is only a simple recruitment platform with no AI CV/Resume scanning or Screening questions, so the process of checking the application by hand still persist]
- The Job Seeker, in their attempt to quickly get a new job, would create multiple account on different platform. And the Employer would face a similar problem where they have to manage multiple platform, and then stitch together applications and decisions by hand.

From my perspective, this is a problem of people not having a centralized platform for the hiring process. And so, I took upon the challenge to create such centralized recruitment platform.

During my internship, my journey with this project begins with studying the fundamentals of Software Development Life Cycle. The implementation process begins with documenting the use case, implementing said use case and if there were any surprises along the way, I would document them back and implement it.

My goal during the development is to learn the skill necessary for Backend Engineering career, hence, I spend my focus on implementing the backend first.
