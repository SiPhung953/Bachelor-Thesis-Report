## 7. "Scary" statistics for the Introduction (supervisor tip)

Goal: open Background/Problem Statement with real numbers that make the reader feel the pain of paper/hand-rolled recruiting — both from the job seeker's and the employer's side.

### Job seeker side (Vietnam-specific data)
- Vietnam labor force 2024: **≈ 52.9–53 million** (participation rate 68.9%, ~51.9M employed) — GSO Labor Force Survey 2024.
- Unemployment ~2.2%, **but youth (15–24) unemployment ~8.2–9%**, ~3–4× the national average (ILO article 2025 / JobOKO report).
- **35% of Vietnamese workers actively seeking a new job** right now; another 21% plan to start hunting within 6 months (Michael Page Vietnam Talent Trends 2023, n=996). 54% more likely to job-hunt when the economy weakens.
- 71.4% of workers are untrained / hold no recognized certification (GSO, via Vietnam Briefing 2025).
- Gen Z ≈ 17% of the workforce; 41% say they would reject a well-paid, qualified offer if the company values don't align (ManpowerGroup).
- Scale of online recruiting in Vietnam (motivates the platform's relevance): TopCV alone reports ~8–10 million candidate profiles and ~540,000 employers.

### Employer side (manual / paper recruiting cost) — mostly global proxies, label as such
- Global average **time-to-hire ~44 days** (AMS + Josh Bersin Q1 2023); easy roles ~14 days, many sit **2–3 months+**; technical roles median ~40–48 days.
- Time from posting to accepted offer averaged **63.5 days** in 2025 (HR Dive / Employ).
- Each hire consumes **~291 applications** on average (Ashby 2024–25 data).
- Recruiters spend **~7 seconds** on an initial resume screen (Ladders eye-tracking 2018; ResumeGo 2024 survey: majority < 1 min).
- Average cost-per-hire ≈ **$5,475** (non-executive, SHRM).

### How to use (draft argument)
"A Vietnamese job seeker today competes in a market where ~35% of workers are job-hunting at the same time; a single opening can draw hundreds of applications that a recruiter glances at for seconds. With a global average of ~44 days from application to offer — longer for difficult roles — the manual, paper round-trip between candidates and employers is exactly the bottleneck a centralized platform removes."

### Caveats
- Only the GSO/Michael Page/TopCV numbers are Vietnam-specific. Time-to-hire (44 days), applications-per-hire (~291), and resume screen (~7 s) are **global** surveys — attribute honestly ("a global survey of recruiters found...").
- Did not find a direct "paper CV by hand vs online, in Vietnam" time-to-hire study. Use the global proxies + the Vietnam seeker/employer supply-demand mismatch instead.
